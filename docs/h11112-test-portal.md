# HSK 1 · H11112

H11112 uses the shared authenticated HSK 1 portal, the same way as H11221. It has eight parts of five questions (20 listening, 20 reading), worked examples, pinyin, server grading, answer explanations and private attempt history. It opens at `/tests/h11112`.

**Status: live.** The bundled file still sets `publishedByDefault: false`, but the paper was published from **Test Studio** in November 2026, once all its pictures were in. It's open to students with approved access.

## Audio and timing

The recording is copied byte for byte to `client/public/hsk1/h11112/listening.mp3`. It's a real MP3 with an ID3 tag. Chromium's `decodeAudioData` measures 921.136 seconds, so listening lasts 922 seconds. The standard 180-second listening review and 1,020-second reading period follow.

| The other 15: `l1-3` … `l1-5`, `l2-6` … `l2-10`, `l3-pictures`, `r1-21` … `r1-25`, `r2-pictures` | Generated by the owner from the prompts in `docs/h11112-artwork-prompts.json`, compressed to WebP (37 MB down to 780 KB) | Cloudinary, uploaded through Test Studio |

**The 15 Cloudinary pictures exist only in the database.** They live in the paper's Test Studio edit, a `PracticePaper` record with id `h11112` and its own `content`. The bundled file `server/data/hsk11112.js` still points at JPG paths that were never added to the repository. As a result:

- **Don't choose "Restore original" on H11112.** It discards the database copy, and the paper goes back to the bundled version with 15 missing pictures.
- **A fresh database doesn't have the pictures.** A new local database, or a migrated one that leaves out `practicepapers`, also gets the version with missing pictures.
- **The permanent fix:** download the 15 WebP files from Cloudinary, convert them to JPG at the sizes in [adding-hsk-mock-test.md](adding-hsk-mock-test.md), and save them as `client/public/hsk1/h11112/<name>.jpg`. Then remove `publishedByDefault: false` and add the image-integrity test that H11221 has.
